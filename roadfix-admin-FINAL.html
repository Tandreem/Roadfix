💡 How the money works

1
UPI / Card / Wallet: customer pays repair + 14% straight to RoadFix. RoadFix then owes the mechanic repair − 10%, paid out at the next monthly settlement.
2
Cash: customer pays the mechanic directly, in full, on the spot — no customer fee. The mechanic then owes RoadFix its 10% commission on that job, settled at day's end / next payout.
3
Your earnings per job = 14% (digital only) + 10% (always) of the repair charge, regardless of which way the cash moves. See Mechanic Payouts for the net amount owed to/from each mechanic.

💵 Mechanic payout — this month, paid on the 27th

1
Every job — cash or digital — carries the same 10% RoadFix fee. Total job value and Platform fee below are added up across ALL of a mechanic's jobs, so you can see exactly how many jobs they did and how much that fee cut comes to.
2
On cash jobs the mechanic already collected the money directly, so that amount is subtracted too (cash kept) — it doesn't need to be paid out again.
3
What's left is the Pay on 27th column — the actual amount to hand each mechanic this month (or, if it's negative, what they still owe you). Click Mark paid once you've actually paid them — this resets their count to zero so the same jobs never get counted twice.
