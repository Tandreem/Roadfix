How the money works

1
UPI / Card / Wallet: customer pays repair + 14% straight to RoadFix. RoadFix then owes the mechanic repair − 10%, paid out at the next monthly settlement.
2
Cash: customer pays the mechanic directly, in full, on the spot — no customer fee. The mechanic then owes RoadFix its 10% commission on that job, settled at day's end / next payout.
3
Your earnings per job = 14% (digital only) + 10% (always) of the repair charge, regardless of which way the cash moves. See Mechanic Payouts for the net amount owed to/from each mechanic.

Weekly mechanic payout — paid every weekend

1
Every job — cash or digital — carries the same 10% RoadFix fee. Total job value and Platform fee below are added up across ALL of a mechanic's jobs since their last payout, so you can see exactly how many jobs they did and how much that fee cut comes to.
2
Mechanics are expected to hand in their cash collections every day (see the reminder in their app). If they did, that amount shows as cash kept and is subtracted here since it doesn't need to be paid out again.
3
If a mechanic doesn't hand in their cash one day, click Missed cash today — this adds a ₹100 penalty and logs a strike (shown next to their name). After 3 strikes you'll be asked if you want to blacklist them.
4
What's left is the Pay this week column — the actual amount to hand each mechanic this weekend (or, if it's negative, what they still owe you). Click Mark paid once you've actually paid them — this resets their balance to zero so the same jobs never get counted twice.
